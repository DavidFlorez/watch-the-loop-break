## Goal

Reproduce the Qwen3.5-0.8B "Weight-Space Programming" result ([Takehi et al., 2026](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/)), confirm that only `layers.15.mlp.down_proj.weight` and `layers.19.mlp.down_proj.weight` differ between base and edited models, and quantify how thinking-token budget (`4,096–32,768`) and continuous weight blending $W(\alpha) = W_{\text{base}} + \alpha(W_{\text{edit}} - W_{\text{base}})$ across $\alpha \in [0, 1.5]$ govern repetition-loop breaking and accuracy on `MATH-500`.

### 7. Loop-Onset Dynamics, Two-Pass Recovery & Metrics Definitions

- **Two complementary loop detectors**:
  - `loop_onset_token` ([`compute_loop_onset_from_token_ids`](metrics.py)): Earliest 0-based token index of a `1,024`-token sliding window (stride `256`) whose decoded whitespace 8-gram repetition ratio strictly exceeds `0.50`. Used for [`assets/article/loop_onset.png`](assets/article/loop_onset.png) and [`assets/article/outcomes_32k.png`](assets/article/outcomes_32k.png).
  - Full-trace cumulative 8-gram repetition `repeated_8gram_ratio(text) >= 0.15` (reported in `looped_pct` in `sweep_results.csv`). At `N=200, B=32768`:
    - `α = 0.00`: `179/200` (**89.5%**) have `loop_onset_token` (median **6,144** tokens; `89.5%` have full-trace `rep >= 0.15`). Once a base trace enters a loop, **`0 / 179` (0.0%) recover** — every single one rides the verbatim loop into the `32,768`-token cap.
    - `α = 1.00`: `66/200` (**33.0%**) have `loop_onset_token` (`46.0%` have full-trace `rep >= 0.15`), with median onset shifted **`+12,288` tokens later to 18,432** on capped traces (`16,128` across all 66). Crucially, **`39 / 66` (59.1%) of looping `α=1.0` traces break out of transient repetition and finish `</think>` cleanly** (`27` capped while looping = `13.5%`, `39` transient recovered = `19.5%`).
    - `α = 1.25`: `51/200` (**25.5%**) have `loop_onset_token` (`38.5%` have full-trace `rep >= 0.15`), median onset shifted to **22,784** tokens on capped traces (`18,944` overall), with **`26 / 51` (51.0%) recovering** (`25` capped = `12.5%`, `26` transient recovered = `13.0%`).
- **Why `4,096` tokens hides the edit**: Across all `200` runs at `α = 1.0`, the median generation length is **`22,088.5` tokens** (`21,742.0` across all `200` runs at `α = 1.25`). Under a `4,096`-token ceiling, `90.5–91.0%` of edited traces are cut mid-thought before `</think>`, capping strict accuracy at `9.0–9.5%`. Even forced `</think>` answer extraction (`Pass 2`) only reaches `17.0–22.5%` at `4k–8k` (compared to `50.0–53.5%` at `32k`), showing that extra thinking tokens produce genuine multi-step mathematical progress rather than mere formatting delay.

## Surprises and limitations

1. **Loop breaking happens early ($\alpha = 0.5$–$1.0$), but coherent `<think>` chains still need `16k–32k` tokens to finish**:
   - Even when repetition loops are eliminated (`0.0%` looped at `α=1.0` under a `4k` ceiling), `Qwen3.5-0.8B`'s reasoning traces have a median length of **`22,088.5` tokens across all `200` runs at `α=1.0`** (`21,742.0` across all `200` runs at `α=1.25`). At `4,096` tokens, `91.0%` (`182/200`) of `α=1.0` runs hit the cap mid-thought (`9.5%` close `</think>`), keeping strict accuracy at `9.0%` (vs `3.0%` Base). Unlocking the budget to `16,384` and `32,768` tokens cuts cap hits to `67.0% -> 31.0%` and raises strict accuracy to **`29.5%` (`3.5×` Base) -> `48.5%` (`5.7×` Base; `51.5%` / `6.1×` at `α=1.25`)**.
2. **More edit cuts loops monotonically (`91% → 17%`), but finished-but-wrong answers rise (`9% → 32%`) and differences across `α ∈ {0.75, 1.0, 1.25}` are within noise**:
   - Across the paired 6-dose sweep (`S_100 ⊂ S_200` @ `32k`), loop prevalence decreases monotonically (`91% -> 72% -> 58% -> 33% -> 22% -> 17%`) and cap hits are non-increasing (`94% -> 63% -> 39% -> 34% -> 34% -> 28%`, flat at `34%` for `α=1.0` and `1.25`), while finished-but-wrong answers increase from `0.0%` (`α=0.0`) and `5.0%` (`α=0.50`) to **`9.0%` (`α=0.75`)**, **`21.0%` (`α=1.0`)**, **`20.0%` (`α=1.25`)**, and **`32.0%` (`α=1.50`)**. Only the step from `α=0.50` to `α=0.75` (`32.0% -> 52.0%`, `+20.0 pp`) is statistically significant (`p ≈ 0.0001`, exact `0.000088`); differences between `α=0.75` and `α=1.0` (`52.0% vs 45.0%`, McNemar `p = 0.14`) and between `α=1.50` and `α=1.0` (`40.0% vs 45.0%`, `p = 0.38`) are within noise (and `52% -> 40%` between `0.75` and `1.50` has `p = 0.0227`). On the full `N=200` cohort, `α=1.25` (`51.5%`) vs `α=1.0` (`48.5%`) is also within noise (`p = 0.3449`).
3. **Why Pass-2 forced answers only partially rescue short budgets (`17–19%` @ `4k` vs `50–53.5%` @ `32k`)**:
   - If the edit merely helped the model wrap up faster, forcing `</think>` at `4,096` tokens would already recover most of the `32k` accuracy. Instead, forced Pass-2 accuracy at `4k` is only `17.0–17.5%` (`α=1.0/1.25`, no better than Base's `19.0%`) and only rises alongside strict reasoning depth (`22.0–22.5%` @ `8k`, `34.5–36.0%` @ `16k`, `50.0–53.5%` @ `32k`). Base (`α=0.0`) stagnates at `19.0% -> 20.0% -> 21.0% -> 21.5%` forced accuracy because once a trace enters a verbatim loop (median onset `6,144` tokens), its KV cache is flooded with thousands of repeated tokens and never recovers (`0/179`).
4. **Transient loops, degenerate whitespace edge case, and seeded legacy `forced_text` omission**:
   - Unlike Base (`0/179 = 0%` recovery once looping), edited models recover from over half of detected loop episodes before `32,768` tokens (`27/58 = 46.6%` at `α=0.75` on `N=100`; `39/66 = 59.1%` at `α=1.0` and `26/51 = 51.0%` at `α=1.25` on `N=200`).
   - One over-edited trace (`α=1.25`, `test/algebra/1578.json`) degenerated into repeated newlines/whitespace to the cap rather than text or `</think>`, and in `assets/gpu_n200/` at `32k`, the `71` unclosed-`</think>` capped rows seeded from legacy `N=50` logs lack `forced_text` in JSONL because the legacy runner did not store raw `forced_text` and only the `12` closed-`</think>` capped `S_50` traces were re-forced (`open_box`).
5. **All GPU results are `bfloat16` on `RTX PRO 6000`; the `T4` `fp16` path is untested**:
   - Every GPU run in this repository executed in native `bfloat16` on an `NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell` (`96 GB`, Compute Capability `12.0`). The Turing `T4` (`sm_75`, `float16` with `mamba_ssm_cache_dtype="float32"` and [`gpu/smoke_test.py`](gpu/smoke_test.py) divergence/NaN gating) path is implemented and unit-tested with mocks, but **has not been executed on physical `T4` hardware**.
